Two designs side by side, under the same traffic and the same failures, with a takeaway computed by the engine.

Monolith vs Microservices, 14 seconds in

  1. Nineteen built-in comparisons

    Monolith vs Microservices, Single DB vs HA Cluster, Sync vs Event-driven, Servers vs Serverless, Brute Force vs CDN + Cache, Single-AZ vs Multi-AZ, Naive Retries vs Resilient Clients, LLM API vs Self-hosted GPUs, Node.js vs Go vs the Database, The Outage After the Fix, Retries at Every Layer, Cells vs One Big Fleet, Queue vs Shed, The Tail at Scale, When Your LLM Provider Goes Down, Peak-sized Fleet vs Autoscaling, One Region vs Two, Read Replicas vs One Bigger Database, a 10 s Pod vs a 2-minute VM. Each one is played in the tests and must show a real difference.

  2. Each side's numbers

    Errors, p99 and $/hour per side; the top bar shows them against each other. Here the orders database just crashed: the monolith fails 99.3% of requests, the microservices 33.1%.

  3. Audit a side

    Score, incidents survived, headroom, cost and the top risks with their fixes, at the scenario's peak load.

  4. Swap a design

    Put any template or your own design on either side.

  5. What is happening

    The scenario's captions as it plays. At the end, the takeaway: what the difference was and why.

  6. The timeline

    The scenario's events (a crash, a surge, a recovery) as marks on its timeline.

From the command line: hindcast compare arch.yaml variants.yaml scenario.yaml ranks variants under one scenario. Share a comparison as a #compare=… link.
