Use cases

Start from your question. Each row says what you need, how to answer it, and where it's explained.

With your real data

Needs metric exports, traces and the bill from a running system. Answers are about your system, with the model's accuracy shown.

#questionhowread
1Will this resize save money without hurting users?cpu: 1 as a strategy, backtested over the last 14 days. A limit below the CPU in use shows up as minutes out of SLO.strategies
2Should we move to Graviton?arch: arm, with perf: from your benchmark.strategies
3Which autoscaling policy: target 60% or 75%, 15-second or 60-second metrics, how much warm-up?One strategy per candidate, ranked side by side; the scaler runs on its own clock.strategies
4Is a replica, a cache or a read replica worth it?replicas, cache, readReplicas: cost against the time out of SLO it removes.backtest
5How much more traffic can we take, and what gives out first?bottleneck headroom; the Model tab's capacities and limits.calibration
6Would retries turn a blip into an outage?retries with and without a budget, per endpoint or all.strategies
7What will this pull request do to production?The ready-made workflow backtests the manifest diff and comments on the PR; --gate fails the check.pull requests
8Would we survive a famous outage?Replay an outage… or bottleneck outage.outages
9Where does the money go, per endpoint and across zones?Endpoints tab: $ per million requests; zone / region strategies price the transfer.endpoints
10Are our critical endpoints inside their own SLOs?Per-endpoint SLOs in the manifest; minutes out of each endpoint's own SLO.data
11What in our telemetry doesn't add up?Fit status and notes per component: unexplained, poor, cost only.calibration
12What was going on at 12:03 on Sep 14?Click the moment: the system replays it on the board. Edit it, backtest the edits.board
13How do I show the result?The HTML report, the PR comment, a card or clip.reports and cards

Without data

A design you draw, write as YAML, or import from Terraform or Kubernetes. Answers are directional: good for comparing designs, finding the weak spot and teaching. Calibrate components from production numbers before making claims about a real system.

#questionhowread
14What breaks first in this design, and what does it cost?The failure audit: a risk register with verified, priced fixes; bottleneck audit in CI.audit
15Which of two designs is better here?Compare: same traffic, same failures, a computed takeaway.compare
16What is the cheapest sizing that meets the SLO?Analysis → cheapest config, or bottleneck optimize.analysis
17How sure is that number?Monte Carlo with 95% confidence intervals.analysis
18How do I learn this?Lessons, and the Live board's next moves.lessons
19How do I walk a room through an incident?Present mode (P); the recorder's incident timeline.sandbox
20Can an AI assistant do this for me?The MCP server: import, audit, compare, optimize, backtest, replay outages; a live canvas inline.MCP
21How do I make a video of it?Studio: episodes rendered to MP4 and carousels.studio
