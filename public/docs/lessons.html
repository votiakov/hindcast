Lessons

Guided levels with an objective the engine checks: keep the system inside its SLO while the traffic, the failures and the bill get worse.

A level

  1. Pick a lesson

    Five lessons, sixteen levels, in order.

  2. The situation

    Each level tells a short story: launch day, the front page of Hacker News, a database brownout, the bill arriving.

  3. The objective

    "Serve 120 req/s with <1% errors and p99 < 300ms for 5s." The engine checks it while you play.

  4. Hold it

    The bar fills while the objective holds; break it and it resets.

  5. Change the design

    Add components from the palette, scale what you have, connect things differently.

  6. Play

    Space plays and pauses. 1×, 2×, 4× speed.
